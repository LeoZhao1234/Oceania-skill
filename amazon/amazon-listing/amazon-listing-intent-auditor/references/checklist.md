# Amazon Listing Intent Audit Checklist

## Fast checklist

### Generalization capture
- Core category appears naturally in title and first bullet.
- Product can still be understood without niche modifiers.
- Broad problem/benefit language is present.

### Specification depth
- Includes material, size, color, fit, compatibility, use case, target user, quantity, and problem-specific phrases where relevant.
- Images prove the specifications visually.
- Bullets move from broad clarity to specific proof.

### Equivalence capture
- Product name, model, SKU, variant, pack count, generation, and compatibility labels are consistent.
- Searchable exact phrases are not split awkwardly.
- Main image and title reinforce the same identity.

### Substitution readiness
- Listing explains why this option is preferable by feature, price, quality, convenience, durability, or use case.
- No unsupported superiority claims.
- Comparison is framed by buyer need, not attacks on competitors.

### Complement opportunity
- Identifies what the product pairs with, fits, refills, protects, replaces, cleans, powers, stores, or upgrades.
- Includes compatible product types and post-purchase accessory language.
- Bundles and variation packs support the complement chain.

### Irrelevant filtering
- First screen makes clear what the product is not for.
- Compatibility boundaries are visible.
- Vague audience claims are replaced by concrete use cases.

## Suggested backend keyword allocation

Default starting point:

- 60% specification long-tail terms
- 15% broad category/generalization terms
- 15% equivalence and substitution terms
- 10% complement terms

Adjust based on actual Search Query Performance, Brand Analytics, PPC query reports, conversion rate, and category norms.

## Rewrite prompts

Use these when producing copy:

- Title: `[core category] + [primary differentiator] + [top specification] + [compatibility/use case]`
- Bullet 1: what it is, who it is for, and the main reason to care.
- Bullet 2: specification proof.
- Bullet 3: compatibility or use-case proof.
- Bullet 4: comparison/value reason.
- Bullet 5: complement, bundle, care, warranty, or risk reversal.
